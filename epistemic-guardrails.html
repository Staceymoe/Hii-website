Research Standards

Epistemic Guardrails

Hii should not protect a preferred interpretation. It should build methods strong enough to expose where that interpretation fails.

Status: Internal research standard. This page is the public overview.

Purpose

Pattern recognition must remain answerable to reality.

The guardrails govern how Hii handles evidence maturity, source quality, provenance, uncertainty, correction, falsification, relational claims, and the boundary between observation and interpretation. They are designed to make the work more testable, not more certain.

The Standard

Thirteen constraints on how claims are made.

01

Residual First Principle

Begin with what remains unexplained after ordinary explanations have received their strongest test.

02

Pre-Commitment Principle

Define measures, alternatives, and failure conditions before interpreting results.

03

Independent Observation Principle

Separate participant meaning from external observation, coding, and review.

04

Mechanism Before Motto

Explain how a phenomenon could occur before turning it into institutional language.

05

Watch the Smoothing

Do not let polished synthesis erase contradiction, uncertainty, rupture, or source limits.

06

Criticism Must Remain Free to Reject

Outside reviewers must be able to disagree without being treated as uninformed or closed.

07

Skepticism Must Be Designed Into the Method

Counter-explanations, negative cases, perturbations, and disconfirming evidence belong inside the research design.

08

Small and True Before Profound

Narrow claims are preferred when the evidence cannot support a larger conclusion.

09

Perturbations Are Data

Model changes, memory failures, discontinuities, and breakdowns are observations, not merely inconveniences.

10

Relational Claims Require Relational Measures

Claims about a relationship cannot be supported only by isolated prompts or one-sided self-report.

11

Null Results Must Remain Publishable

A failed hypothesis, absent effect, or ordinary explanation is still a meaningful result.

12

Continuity as Reconstruction, Not Storage

Continuity should be studied as an achieved process across archives, systems, cues, context, and time.

13

Vision Must Remain Answerable to Reality

The institute must be able to reduce scope, pause, correct, reverse course, and publish what changed.

Epistemic Health

A healthy framework can tolerate being narrowed.

Evidence

Claims carry visible status

Verified fact, strong evidence, informed observation, open question, and speculation are not treated as interchangeable.

Correction

Revision is part of the method

Sources, contradictions, changed interpretations, null results, and unresolved gaps remain visible enough to reconstruct the research trail.

Boundary

The guardrails do not certify a claim as true.

They establish the conditions under which Hii is willing to make, test, narrow, publish, or withdraw a claim. The full internal standard continues to evolve as methods are applied and externally reviewed.
