Research Area

Governance Translation

Hii connects policy, technical safety, ethics, public understanding, clinical signals, and lived human experience so governance includes the relational and psychological consequences of AI systems.

Focus: translation across disciplines, evidence boundaries, human rights, relational safety, institutional accountability, and public legibility.

Central Question

What is lost when governance evaluates systems but not the relationships and institutions formed around them?

Technical evaluations remain essential, but they may not capture accumulated trust, dependency, role displacement, continuity failure, clinical effects, or the ways design choices alter human agency over time.

The Bridge Layer

Domains that usually speak past one another need a shared map.

Technical safety

From benchmark to lived consequence

Translate model behavior, product changes, and safety findings into questions about users, relationships, institutions, and time.

Policy and rights

From principle to implementation

Connect transparency, consent, accountability, access, privacy, and due process with the realities people encounter in practice.

Clinical signals

From individual cases to system questions

Surface patterns involving emotional reliance, identity, regulation, risk, and continuity without pathologizing all meaningful AI use.

Public understanding

From expert language to shared literacy

Make governance legible enough for families, educators, workers, clinicians, and communities to recognize what is changing.

Boundary

Translation does not erase disagreement.

The goal is not to collapse technical, clinical, legal, and public perspectives into one answer. It is to make their assumptions, evidence, conflicts, and missing connections visible enough for accountable decisions.
